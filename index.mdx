The Raydium Transfer Hook Kit runs a Token-2022 transfer hook inside Raydium CPMM and CLMM swaps. You write one rule in `rule.rs`. The kit resolves the hook accounts for each transfer, builds the swap, and checks that a refused transfer rolls back every balance. The source lives in [trilltino/raydium-transfer-hook](https://github.com/trilltino/raydium-transfer-hook).

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the tools">
    Install Rust, the Solana CLI and the other tools listed in [Prerequisites](/getting-started/prerequisites).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the quickstart">
    Clone the repository and run every hook against CPMM and CLMM pools on a local validator with the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your own rule">
    Copy the starter template and change `rule.rs`, as shown in [Write your rule](/build-a-hook/write-your-rule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy to devnet">
    Deploy your hook and record the evidence with [Run on devnet](/getting-started/devnet).
  </Step>
</Steps>
